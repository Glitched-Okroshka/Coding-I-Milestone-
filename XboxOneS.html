About the Xbox One S
The Xbox One S is the newer version of the Xbox One and the fourth console made by Microsoft in 2016. The Xbox One S was the revised, high-end version of the Xbox One, that is smaller and is the design finally gets rid of that "gigantic power brick" look. The Xbox One S gives display services up to 4k quality in video in streaming and Ultra HD Blu-rays and more things showing it was better than its non-upgraded version (Xbox One,) and considered a better fit to compete against the PlayStation4 by Sony. Even though all of these changes do sound worth the buy, it was never enough to be considered a better alternative if someone already owned the Xbox One, leaving it in the dust in sales and a massive disappointment compared to the other Xbox consoles.
