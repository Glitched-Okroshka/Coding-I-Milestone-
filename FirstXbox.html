About the First Xbox
The first xbox was released in 2001, over a decade ago now, and was immediately put in competition with the Playstation 2 and GameCube The Xbox was created as a diverse strategy in order to capitalize in the gaming industry at the time and compete due to the concerns of the successful PlayStation 2 possibly pushing out the personal computer market. Due to being the first Xbox, the new later models coming out would introduce more power an capability than the first, however at that time it produced more than twice the power than the Ps2, pushing it high up in the market as just the first console of its kind.
