About the XBox One
The Xbox One is the third home video game console in the Xbox franchise and was also the first Xbox to be released in China. Developed in 2013, it was now in the competition with Sony's PlayStation 4 and Nintendo's Wii U and soon the Nintendo Switch. The aspects of the Xbox One were mostly taken from the previous Xbox consoles One and the original Xbox but still received positive reviews for their controller designs. Diving into the console, the Xbox One had an "increased emphasis" on social networking and recording and video. This gave the Xbox One the ability to clip gameplay and or livestream directly to the certain streaming services like Mixer and Twitch at the time.
