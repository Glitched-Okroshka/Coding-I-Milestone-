About the Xbox 360
The Xbox 360 was a home video console created and published by Microsoft in 2005 and is the second of its kind from the original Xbox design. This new Xbox generation provided new exclusive features of the Xbox including the new Xbox live system that allowed its users to play games online, download games and game demos, purchase and stream music, access third-party content services through media streaming applications, and more. Updates were discontinued after the development of the Xbox One, but the Xbox 360 still had many important achievements in gaming ability with just one feature.
